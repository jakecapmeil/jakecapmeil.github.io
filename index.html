01 Research

Mentored Research

Independent study conducted under faculty mentorship.

Carnegie Mellon University · Summer 2026

Threshold Tuning Under Class Imbalance for Predicting Student Preference in AI-Assisted Tutoring

Mentored research at Carnegie Mellon's CS Scholars Program modeling student preference for AI versus human tutoring. I compared three classifiers using k-fold cross-validation, optimized for MCC, and applied threshold tuning to correct for class imbalance in a small self-collected dataset. It stands as a proof of concept for prediction under the data constraints most institutions actually face.
